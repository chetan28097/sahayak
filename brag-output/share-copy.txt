SUBMISSION: WRITTEN PROJECT EXPLANATION
---------------------------------------
Sahayak is a voice-first bank kiosk for people who find bank forms hard: elderly customers, people with low literacy, and people who aren't comfortable in English. The customer chooses Kannada, Hindi or English and says what they need. Sahayak asks one question at a time and reads every value back for a spoken "yes". It fills the actual bank form: pay-in slip, AOF + CKYC + DA-1 nomination, PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY enrolment, KYC update or service request. The customer gets a token and an AES-256-GCM-encrypted, HMAC-signed QR. A separate branch console lets named staff verify each checklist item, ask the customer for more information, escalate, reject or complete the request, with every action recorded in an audit log.

Gnani AI technology used:
- Vachana STT (api.vachana.ai/stt/v3) transcribes the customer's speech in Kannada, Hindi, English and code-mixed Hinglish. Browser audio is resampled to 16 kHz mono WAV and capped below the 30 s limit.
- Timbre TTS (v3.0, voice "Shivani") speaks every prompt and read-back. Fixed prompts are pre-generated and cached on disk to save credits.

Rules always run first; an LLM is only a fallback for understanding free-form answers. No audio is stored, transcripts are masked, and Aadhaar appears only as its last 4 digits. All demo data is synthetic.
